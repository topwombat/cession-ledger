# The Cession Ledger

How Native land passed to the United States, treaty by treaty, 1784 to 1893, and what the government's own Indian Claims Commission later found it was worth against what was paid.

**Live page:** https://topwombat.github.io/cession-ledger/

A prototype from the *Magnalia Americana* channel's research. Drag the year to watch the land change hands; switch to **By price** to see the gaps; click any area for its treaty, nations, date and, where the Commission ruled, its value at the time of taking against the consideration paid, with the decisions cited. Link straight to an area with `#area-<Royce number>`, for example [`#area-72`](https://topwombat.github.io/cession-ledger/#area-72) (Fort Wayne, 1809) or [`#area-75`](https://topwombat.github.io/cession-ledger/#area-75) (Fort Jackson, 1814).

## What the figures are

- **Areas** are Charles C. Royce's numbered cession areas (*Indian Land Cessions in the United States*, 18th Annual Report of the Bureau of American Ethnology, 1899), drawn from the US Forest Service's digital layer of Royce's maps. Acreages on the map are computed from the simplified shapes.
- **Worth** is the Indian Claims Commission's finding, made decades later in litigation, of what the land was worth on the date it was taken. **Paid** is the Commission's finding of the consideration, with annuities converted as the Commission did. They are adjudicated figures, not market prices. Every card names the decision, volume and page.
- **N/A** means no Commission valuation was found. For the removal-era cessions of the Cherokee, Choctaw and Chickasaw east of the Mississippi this is expected: those claims were litigated before the Commission existed (for example *Choctaw Nation v. United States*, 119 U.S. 1, 1886).
- **∞** means the Commission valued the land and nothing was paid for it (Fort Jackson, 1814).

## Coverage so far

Prices for Indiana, Ohio, Illinois, Michigan and Alabama, with the Cherokee and Mississippi rows (mostly N/A, for the reason above). Not yet included: other states; Royce's Georgia, Tennessee, Iowa 2, Illinois 1 and three Indian Territory maps, which have no digital areas yet; cessions before 1784; and what the government later took in from each area.

## Sources

- Cession areas: US Forest Service, Indian land cessions layer (after Royce 1899), public domain.
- Treaty texts: Charles J. Kappler, *Indian Affairs: Laws and Treaties*, via Oklahoma State University Library's digital collection.
- Valuations: decisions of the Indian Claims Commission (1946-1978), via Oklahoma State University Library's digital collection; public domain.
- State outlines: [us-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas) by Mike Bostock (ISC licence), from the US Census Bureau.
- Native voices: Tecumseh's speech at Vincennes, August 1810, as printed in R. David Edmunds, *The Shawnee Prophet* (1983); *Life of Ma-ka-tai-me-she-kia-kiak, or Black Hawk* (1833).
- Type: Spectral and Libre Franklin (SIL Open Font Licence), loaded from Google Fonts. Map drawing: D3 and TopoJSON, loaded from cdnjs.
